ABOUT THE PROJECT

A path towards
information and access.

Ka Lynti is an independent information initiative being developed with Meghalaya in focus.

Our purpose

Bridging the gap between knowing and accessing.

Finding reliable information can be difficult, especially when it is scattered across departments, organisations and websites.

Ka Lynti aims to bring useful information together in clear language and help people identify possible next steps.

The platform is intended for disabled people, families, caregivers, educators and anyone who wants to learn more about disability and inclusion.

Our commitments

  • Respect disabled people's autonomy and dignity.
  • Use clear language and make accessibility part of the design.
  • Distinguish verified information from information still being researched.
  • Prioritise local relevance without inventing contacts or services.
  • Review time-sensitive information and show when it was checked.
