Understand disability

Start with clear, respectful information.

Explore disability-related language, broad groupings and support needs. These categories are starting points for learning, not labels that define a person.

An important distinction

A person may have overlapping disabilities and support needs. A single category should never be used to assume what someone can or cannot do. These broad educational groupings are not the nine categories under Indian disability law.
